# Security policy

Itaja moves money. We take every report seriously and thank you for making one.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Do not open a public issue, discussion or pull request.**

- In a public repository of this organisation: open the **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability** (GitHub private vulnerability reporting).
- Otherwise, or for the hosted service (`getitaja.com`, `api.getitaja.com`, the dashboard, the pay pages, the customer portal): write to [hello@getitaja.com](mailto:hello@getitaja.com) with the subject `SECURITY`, and we will move the conversation to a private channel.

Please include what is affected, the steps to reproduce, the impact you observed, and how to reach you.

## What to expect

- Acknowledgement within 3 business days.
- A first assessment within 10 business days, then updates until the fix ships.
- Credit in the advisory if you wish, once the fix is released.

## Rules of engagement

- Test only against **sandbox** keys (`itjs_…`) and accounts you own. Never use a live key, never move real money, never access, change or delete another merchant's or customer's data.
- No denial of service, spam, social engineering or physical attacks.
- Give us reasonable time to fix before any disclosure.

Good-faith research that follows these rules will not be pursued.

## Supported versions

The hosted service is always the current version. For the SDKs and the agent skills, the latest release receives security fixes.
